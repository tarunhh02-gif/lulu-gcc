Questions about an order

Have your order number ready — it is in your confirmation email and under Orders in the app. With it we can answer most questions in a single reply. Without it we usually need to ask for it first, which costs you a round trip.

  • Something is missing or wrong — send a photo of what arrived along with the order number. We can usually resolve it without a return.
  • Order has not arrived — check the tracking link first; if the status has not moved in [ STALE TRACKING WINDOW ], contact us.
  • You want to change or cancel — see Shipping & delivery for the cut-off, after which an order is already being picked.
  • Refund not received — refunds take [ REFUND WINDOW ] to appear depending on your bank. See Returns policy.

Business, bulk and partnerships

For wholesale pricing, catering volumes, corporate gifting or supplying us as a vendor, email business@lulu-kw-supermarket.app rather than general support — it goes straight to the commercial team.

Include what you need, rough volumes and how often. Vague enquiries take several exchanges to price; a specific one usually gets a quote in the first reply.

Data and privacy requests

To access, correct, or delete the personal data we hold about you, or to withdraw consent, write to privacy@lulu-kw-supermarket.app. Full detail on what we collect and your rights is in our Privacy policy.

We may ask you to confirm your identity before acting on a request — this protects your account from someone else making the request on your behalf.
